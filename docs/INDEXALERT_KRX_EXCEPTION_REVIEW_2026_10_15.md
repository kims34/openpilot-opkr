## Implemented: pure-Python compatibility checks, not original source admission

`research_v1_krx_offline_exception_review.py` is an **offline receipt-format and contradiction checker**. The isolated `test_research_v1_krx_offline_exception_review.py` includes synthetic positive and negative cases with **no real symbol, price, receipt, holiday calendar or account**. Explicit `exception_dates` must be unique sorted 2017–2022 weekdays and match SHA-shaped private `raw_day` source receipts exactly.

- Optional independently claimed calendar receipts may label only `OPEN` or `CLOSED`. A closed session with source trade rows, or an open session with zero source rows, **fails closed** instead of being treated as a verified holiday or market gap. Missing day claims stay unresolved.
- Optional security/day receipts may label `SUSPENDED`, `DELISTED`, `LISTED`, or `UNKNOWN`, and must match SHA-only quarantined nonpositive-OHLC security/day tokens exactly. They **never** authorize fills even if status is `LISTED`.
- Claims carry strict per-source 64-digit SHA-256 format and explicit source category. Such metadata **is not authenticated by this local function**, and no external publisher or as-of-vintage signature is verified by this candidate. This function deliberately returns `calendar_original_publication_pit_verified=false`, `individual_tradability_verified=false`, `historical_corporate_actions_verified=false`, `full_universe_source_admitted=false`, `m20_performance_run_authorized=false`, `live_orders_authorized=false`, `ordering=DISABLED` regardless of apparent consistency.
- No actual list of 90 dates, individual 27 securities, full source hashes, raw rows, market close prices or brokerage identifiers appears in GitHub Actions. GitHub unit-test success proves only **synthetic guard logic**, not completeness/accuracy of KRX historical calendar or any real market-data admission.

## 2026-10-15 KST — authoritative 2021 calendar revision explains the 13-vs-10 annual count discrepancy (still NOT PIT)

**New independent source research:** Railway's actual KRX private 2017–2022 integrity marker has the following **calendar/source-day exception counts**, year by year: 2017=17, 2018=17, 2019=15, 2020=14, **2021=13**, 2022=14 (total=90). Exactly **1,565/1,565** raw daily/master weekday source pairs pass SHA/schema storage verification; per-date official exchange status remains unverified. Historical KRX 2020-12-28 announcement for calendar 2021 initially specified **251 stock trading days and 10 weekday closures** (reported contemporaneously: https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20201225031900002). Crucially the **2021-08-04 KRX substitute-holiday update**, published contemporaneously, announced additional stock-market closure dates **2021-08-16, 2021-10-04, 2021-10-11** (https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20210804093200002). Independent official civil-law announcement: https://openminister.mpm.go.kr/mpm/comm/newsPress/newsPressRelease/?boardId=bbs_0000000000000029&cntId=3249&mode=view (2021-08-03).

**Thus the apparent 2021 three-day count surplus has a specific plausible official closure cause: originally planned 10 + three later-announced closures = 13, exactly matching the private storage-only count 13.** This is **not proof** those exact private 2021 source-gap dates are the three substitute holidays; obtaining their actual SHA-bound dates and matching the post-announcement stock-market schedule is mandatory. Do not declare 2021 cleared or the remaining 90 admitted based on annual totals or an obsolete initial-year calendar. The closure notice was published **2021-08-04**; any PIT-relevant availability must not be retroactively assigned to an earlier date. This is a useful distinction for historical calendar vintage tests.

- [Code `231998d0`](https://github.com/kims34/openpilot-opkr/commit/231998d01749a996f6fb4f466b895216b5405277) adds strictly three fixed later KRX closure dates and the 2021-08-04 notice date as an **offline contradiction gate**: if the actual SHAs-only private captured-day claim contains one of those dates but also claims a positive source stock-trade row count, reject immediately; likewise reject an `OPEN` official-date metadata claim for the date. No actual raw source or exchange provider route fetched by the code, and metadata claims are still **untrusted until independently authenticated**.

## 2026-10-20 KST — KRX *equity market* session evidence scope explicitly required

**Discovered actionable defect:** The offline exceptional-day checker previously accepted user-supplied market-calendar claims using only the marker `source_kind="INDEPENDENT_OFFICIAL_SESSION_CLAIM"` plus a SHA-shaped string. It did not carry **which KRX market** the purported source concerned. A real historic KRX derivatives/commodities holiday notice (for example the [2021-09-27 KRX derivatives announcement](https://kind.krx.co.kr/external/2021/09/27/000115/20210927000265/70868.htm)) could therefore inadvertently be labeled as proof of KOSPI securities holiday despite the markets having different applicability. The archived [2021-08-04 KRX *유가증권시장* notice](https://kind.krx.co.kr/external/2021/08/04/000158/20210804000347/99340.htm) correctly covers equities; [contemporaneous KRX announcement reported by Yonhap at 13:52 KST](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20210804093200002) distinguishes equities/ETF/ETN, derivatives and commodities.

**Fix:** [`511895a8`](https://github.com/kims34/openpilot-opkr/commit/511895a8d6a5f79e685379e9866d2a2ae3e8709c) makes the independent `calendar` record require an **explicit fixed `market_scope="KOSPI_EQUITIES"`** together with the existing `date`, `session`, `evidence_sha256` and `source_kind`. Missing scope, ambiguous `ALL_MARKETS`, `KRX_DERIVATIVES`, ETF issuer product disclosures, or nonstrings fail closed; no attempt is made to fabricate authoritative exchange-specific proof from a claim alone. **Review output schema bumped to `indexalert-krx-offline-exception-review-v2`** so an upstream owner cannot silently apply the legacy unscoped receipt contract. The existing 2017–2022 versus 2026-09-28 horizons and retrospective no-PIT/no-tradability/no-order flags remain unchanged.
